Machine learning algorithms process vast streams of numerical data to identify hidden patterns and make accurate predictions. In natural language processing, text is tokenized into smaller sub-word units and mapped into high-dimensional vector spaces. Transformer architectures rely heavily on self-attention mechanisms to weigh the importance of every token relative to all other tokens in the sequence. By scaling both model parameters and training dataset lengths, developers can significantly improve a system's ability to retain context across long documents. However, managing memory complexity and positional encodings remains a critical engineering challenge during extended pre-training phases.
